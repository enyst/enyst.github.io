Do not overwrite the published evidence. Runs are not byte-reproducible: model
aliases, provider deployments and stochastic responses can change.

The subscription endpoint does not accept temperature or max-output-token caps.
Luna low reasoning is explicitly passed through litellm_extra_body.reasoning and
verified in the SDK's normalized Responses options. Server interpretation is not
independently attested. Both other models request temperature0 and256 output
tokens, with legacy Anthropic thinking disabled and reasoning effort none.
All arms use the unchanged50-character SDK display cap.

Before scoring, exact duplicate(case_id, displayed_title) pairs were deduplicated.
Blinded scorer inputs include full request, displayed title, family and split;
exclude model/arm identities and generation prompts. Unique outputs are shuffled
with seed10302026 and divided round-robin into three batches. One separate batch
judges all family groups. All use Codex/gpt-6-astra medium, read-only. Exact
scoring instructions are published as scoring-titles.txt and scoring-groups.txt.
The same unique grade maps back to all its occurrences. AI judgments are not
measurements of human reading speed or statistical proof.
